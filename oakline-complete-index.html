The Oakline Process

A better way to solve vehicle problems.

A trouble code identifies the system that detected a problem. It does not automatically identify the failed part.

1

Verify the concern

We confirm the symptoms and collect the information needed to begin testing correctly.

2

Test the system

Scan data, electrical measurements and system testing are used to isolate the actual fault.

3

Explain the findings

You receive a clear explanation and an honest recommendation before repair decisions are made.
