Automotive Electrical Diagnostics

Modern electrical problems can affect several systems at once and may be intermittent. Oakline tests the circuit under the conditions where it fails instead of replacing parts based on a code or symptom alone.

Request ServiceCall Oakline

When You May Need This Service

Oakline provides mobile testing at homes and workplaces throughout Fayetteville and nearby communities.

Common symptoms

  • Battery repeatedly goes dead
  • Intermittent warning lights or accessories
  • Blown fuses, shorts or open circuits
  • Modules that lose communication

What Oakline checks

  • Battery and charging-system condition
  • Power, ground and voltage-drop testing
  • Current draw and parasitic-drain testing
  • Wiring, connector and network integrity

Diagnosis Before Parts

The goal is to identify the failed circuit, connection or component and prevent unnecessary module or parts replacement.
