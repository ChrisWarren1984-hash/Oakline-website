Common Questions

What does the $89 diagnostic include?

The initial mobile visit, professional system scan, live-data review and initial diagnostic testing. More involved testing is discussed before additional charges.

Do you repair the vehicle afterward?

Select mobile repairs may be available after diagnosis depending on the job, equipment required and work location.

Do you only read trouble codes?

No. Codes are clues. Testing may include live data, bi-directional controls, circuit checks, voltage-drop testing and component verification.
