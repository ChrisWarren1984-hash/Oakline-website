Common Questions

What does the $89 diagnostic include?

It includes the initial mobile visit, a professional system scan, live-data review and initial diagnostic testing. More involved electrical or mechanical testing is discussed before additional charges.

Do you repair the vehicle after diagnosing it?

Select mobile repairs may be available after diagnosis. Repair availability depends on the vehicle, required equipment and work location.

Do you only read trouble codes?

No. Codes are used as clues. Testing may include live data, bi-directional controls, circuit checks, voltage-drop testing and component verification.

What areas do you serve?

Oakline serves Fayetteville and nearby communities including Hope Mills, Raeford, Spring Lake, Stedman and Eastover. Contact Oakline to confirm your location.
