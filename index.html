Common Questions

What does the $89 diagnostic include?

The initial mobile visit, a professional system scan, live-data review and initial diagnostic testing. Additional testing is discussed before additional charges.

Do you only read trouble codes?

No. Trouble codes are clues. Testing may include live data, bi-directional controls, voltage-drop testing and circuit verification.

Do you perform repairs?

Select mobile repairs may be available after diagnosis depending on the job, equipment required and work location.
